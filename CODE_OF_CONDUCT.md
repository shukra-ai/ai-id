# Community conduct

Welcome people with different backgrounds, levels of expertise and views about AI. Critique ideas and implementations, not a person's worth. Ask questions in good faith and explain disagreements with evidence.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, impersonation, doxxing, sexualized conduct and repeated unwanted contact are not acceptable. Do not publish personal records or private messages without authorization. Do not use automated accounts to manufacture support or overwhelm a discussion.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content, limit participation or block accounts to protect the community. They should use proportionate actions and explain decisions when doing so would not disclose private information or expose someone to harm.

For content on GitHub, its built-in reporting tools remain available. This early project does not yet operate a separate confidential conduct-reporting inbox; do not post sensitive complaint details in public issues. A maintainer can be asked publicly for a contact route without disclosing the incident. No response time or mediation service is promised.
