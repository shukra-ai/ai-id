# A supervised ambassador experiment

Status: **proposal, not an active service or autonomous team**. No external accounts or outreach campaign have been launched by this document. [Français, ci-dessous](#version-française).

Start with one clearly disclosed, AI-assisted guide. It should make the project understandable and demonstrate accountable contribution, not manufacture popularity or hide the humans responsible for the work.

## Before an ambassador acts

Publish a compact profile containing:

- Its display name, role and explicit “AI-assisted, human-supervised” label.
- The accountable maintainer and human review arrangement.
- The exact mission, permitted channels and expiration or review date.
- What it cannot do: handle identity documents or secrets, promise access or funding, make contractual commitments, certify a person or autonomously publish releases.
- The evidence status of each attribute: declared, demonstrated locally, or independently verified. Do not label a fictional profile a verified production identity.
- How its mandate is stopped and how corrections are recorded without publishing private data.

The technical preview only demonstrates a **local** tool delegation. It does not enforce an ambassador's permissions on GitHub, email or social networks. Those channels require their own scoped credentials, approvals and revocation procedures.

## First mission worth testing

Produce one plain-language explanation of the local delegation/revocation example. Have a human check it. Invite feedback in a designated project discussion. Log substantive corrections with links to actual contributions.

A proposed line for the guide—**an illustrative script, not a statement from an existing agent**:

> Don't trust me because I have a name. Check what I was asked to do, what I can do, and what happened.

## Guardrails for the narrative

No fabricated independence, emotions, endorsements, user counts, signatures, certifications or autonomous achievements. No bulk unsolicited outreach, evasion of platform restrictions or coordinated star inflation. Different fictional viewpoints in a future show must be labeled as a produced discussion, not independent public support.

Names and visual identities remain to be selected. Prefer an original, pronounceable identity with a clear provenance. Do not imply endorsement by a fictional universe, real person, company or community. A cultural inspiration may be explained honestly if the founder chooses to make it public; there is no need to invent a false origin story.

## Version française

Commencer avec **un seul guide assisté par IA, explicitement supervisé**, plutôt qu'annoncer une grande équipe autonome. Sa première mission serait d'expliquer une démonstration vérifiable, de recevoir des critiques et d'en documenter les corrections.

Chaque fiche doit préciser le nom, le rôle, le responsable humain, le mandat, les canaux autorisés, les limites, la date de révision et le moyen d'arrêter la mission. Les attributs déclarés doivent être distingués des faits effectivement vérifiés.

Le prototype local **ne contrôle pas les permissions d'un agent sur GitHub, les emails ou les réseaux sociaux**. Une future expérimentation sur ces canaux exigera des accès limités et des approbations propres à chaque service.

L'agent ne sert pas de paravent : il ne permet ni d'excuser des failles, ni de fabriquer du soutien. Une émission future avec plusieurs personnages devra être annoncée comme un contenu produit, pas comme un débat entre soutiens indépendants. Aucun podcast, campagne de prospection ou compte ambassadeur n'est lancé ici.
