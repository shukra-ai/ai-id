# Governance

Status: initial maintainer-led project. The accountable publishing and maintenance account is `shukra-ai`. This document does not claim a foundation, independent board, standards-body status or existing external adoption.

## Decision process

Small fixes use issues and pull requests. Material protocol, security, privacy or compatibility changes begin with a proposal describing the problem, alternatives, threat/privacy implications and acceptance tests. Maintainers publish a rationale and record accepted decisions. A request for comments is not an assertion of community consensus.

Use these document states: **Idea**, **Draft**, **Experimental**, **Candidate**, **Stable**, **Deprecated**. “Accepted architecture” describes a design decision; it does not certify its implementation. Nothing in this initial preview is a stable interoperability guarantee.

Security reports and personal information remain private where an appropriate channel exists. Publish sanitized lessons and corrected specifications, not exploit details before coordinated remediation or identifiable incident records.

## People and agents

Human maintainers are responsible for releases and project decisions. An AI-assisted ambassador or documentation persona must be identified as such, with a human sponsor, bounded mandate and review process. Personas cannot confer certification, independently govern the project or make contractual commitments.

No character, fictional affiliation, endorsement or organizational partnership is implied by this repository. Any future ambassador program will be documented before it is represented as active.

## Becoming more open over time

External maintainers and shared governance should follow sustained contributions, demonstrated responsibility and explicit conflict-of-interest handling. Publish proposed governance changes for discussion. Stars, donations and early signup order do not buy influence over trust decisions.
